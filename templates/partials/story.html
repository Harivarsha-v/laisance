I

The cloth

It begins in Biella, where the mills have spun wool for six centuries. We choose each bolt by hand: double-face cashmere, two layers woven as one, so the coat needs no lining at all.

II

The leather

From a family tannery in Tuscany, box calfskin is tanned slowly with bark and time. It arrives stiff and pale, and softens to your hand over years, not days.

III

The hand

Every collar is shaped over the knee and every buttonhole sewn by hand, some forty minutes each. A single coat passes through eleven pairs of hands before it is finished.

IV

The atelier

Behind the salon, our workroom keeps the patterns of every piece we have made. Nothing is cut by machine, and nothing leaves until it falls exactly as it should.

V

Paris

Made in small numbers, in the house where it was drawn. Then it is yours, to be worn for decades and still feel effortless.
